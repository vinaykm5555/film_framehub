Studio Policies & FAQs

What is your booking advance and payment schedule? expand_more

To guarantee your wedding date, a 50% reservation advance is held securely inside FrameHub Escrow. 40% is due on the primary wedding day, and the final 10% is released exclusively after you approve your master photo gallery and edited cinematic documentary.

Do you travel outside Hyderabad across Andhra Pradesh or destination venues? expand_more

Yes! We regularly travel across Vijayawada, Vizag, Tirupati, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Rajasthan. For outstation assignments, round-trip flight/train transit and comfortable hotel lodging for the assigned crew are arranged by the client or billed transparently at actuals.

What happens if dates need to be postponed due to unforeseen reasons? expand_more

Notice provided 60+ days prior allows one date transfer at zero fee (subject to team availability within 6 months). For cancellations, 100% of advance is refunded if provider declines within 24 hours under FrameHub Guarantee.

Can we choose our background songs for the teaser film? expand_more

Absolutely. Before the edit begins, our lead editor shares a music curation form where you can highlight favorite tracks, nostalgic regional songs, or let our sound designers propose custom royalty-cleared cinematic scores.
